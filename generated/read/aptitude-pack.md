PLACEMENT TESTS · APTITUDE

# 150 aptitude questions with worked answers

Many fresher online assessments, especially at service companies and in campus first rounds, start with an aptitude section. This pack has 60 quantitative, 50 logical reasoning and 40 verbal questions at that level. Answers with a worked reason are on separate pages at the end.

**Timing.** Quant about 75 seconds a question, logical about 45, verbal about 30. Do a section in one sitting with a timer. Skip any question after its time is up and come back at the end; one stuck question costs three easy ones. Aim for 80% before moving on, and log every miss with the shortcut you missed.

## How the answers were checked

- Every quantitative answer (60 of 60) and every computable reasoning answer (series, codes, directions, clocks, calendars, rankings, sets: 32 of 50) was recomputed in Python and matches the key (`generated/src/test_apti.py`).
- The other reasoning items (relations, syllogisms, analogies) and all verbal items were written and reviewed by hand; each has its reason in the key.
- Answer positions are shuffled: A 43, B 35, C 34, D 38.

Questions and explanations are original. Free practice: the aptitude sections on IndiaBix and PrepInsta, and previous campus papers from your seniors.
