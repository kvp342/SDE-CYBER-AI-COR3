## 01. How to use this (read once)

- **One row per week.** Look at this week's row on Monday; that is all you need to plan the week.
- **Exact problems for today:** open the Playbook (playbook.html, Today tab) or continue on [Codolio](https://codolio.com/question-tracker/sheet/strivers-a2z-dsa-sheet)^([\[1\]](#-sources)) from the first unticked problem. This table only says which A2Z steps the week should finish.
- **Every day:** DSA 3–4 h (A2Z problems + 1–2 LeetCode on the same topic), Cloud 1 h, Core CS 1 h, Anki 15 min.
- **Behind?** Never double up on weekdays. Use Sunday afternoon to catch up, and if a week slips, slide the rest; the 30 Jan A2Z checkpoint can move by a week without harm.
- **Cyber:** Saturdays 2 h until A2Z is done, then daily from February. The topic column comes from the Playbook's cyber track.
- **Deadlines and contests** are also in your Google Calendar. LeetCode weekly contests run on Sundays at 8:00 am IST.^([\[2\]](#-sources))
