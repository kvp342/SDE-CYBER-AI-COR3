# 90 more aptitude questions with worked answers

A second aptitude set at fresher-OA level: 40 quantitative questions on topics the first pack did not cover (alligation, work and wages, leaks, relative speed, half-yearly compounding, depreciation, factors and remainders, circular arrangements, cards, mensuration, progressions), 30 logical reasoning questions (new series, codes, calendars, clocks, a seating puzzle, cube and grid counting, syllogisms), and 20 data-interpretation questions in four sets (a sales table, a budget split, placement rates, monthly users). Answers with a worked reason are on separate pages at the end.

**Timing.** Quant about 75 seconds a question, logical about 45, verbal about 30. Do a section in one sitting with a timer. Skip any question after its time is up and come back at the end; one stuck question costs three easy ones. Aim for 80% before moving on, and log every miss with the shortcut you missed.
