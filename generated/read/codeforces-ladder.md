# Codeforces 800 → 1600

Your plan's HFT gate on 31 March 2027 is a Codeforces rating of 1400 or more (or LeetCode contest 1800+). This ladder gives each core topic five rating bands, each a link to Codeforces' own problem set filtered by that topic and rating, plus a six-week plan that fits the plan's CP habit: two problems on Tuesday and Thursday, and one round at the weekend.

**Be realistic.** Rating moves only through rated contests, and each round shifts it by tens of points, not hundreds. If you want a real chance at 1400 by 31 March, start taking a Div 3 or Div 4 round every two weeks from January so your rating exists before February. Missing the gate is fine: you stay on Path A.

| 5    | 08 Mar – 14 Mar | **DP, graphs**Tue and Thu: 2 problems each in 1200–1400 · weekend: one round                  | ☐ Upsolve every problem up to 1400 from your round.                         |

Exams run 22 March to 7 April: the CP habit pauses (minimum day only). The gate is checked on Wednesday 31 March from your current rating. If Codeforces rounds clash with exams, skip them; one missed round does not change the decision much.

## How to practise

- **Time-box:** 30 minutes on a problem below your level, 45 at your level. Then read the editorial, close it, and write the solution yourself.
- **Upsolve:** after every round, solve the next problem you could not finish. This is where most improvement comes from.
- **Log:** add each problem you needed help on to your revision tracker; redo it after 3, 7 and 21 days like the DSA problems.
- **Speed:** Div 3 problems A and B should take under 15 minutes together by week 4. Typing a clean template (fast I/O, long long) saves minutes: see the STL card and the 30 C++ bugs.
- **Rounds:** Div 4 and Div 3 rounds suit 800–1400. Educational rounds and Div 2 are harder; try them after 1300. Virtual contests count for practice but not for rating.
- **Hacks and penalties:** wrong submissions cost penalty time; test on edge cases (n = 1, all equal, maximum values) before submitting.

### Rating, in short

Your rating changes after each rated round based on your rank against the ratings of other participants: beating the expectation raises it. New accounts start low and climb fastest in the first few rounds, so start rounds early rather than practising only offline.
